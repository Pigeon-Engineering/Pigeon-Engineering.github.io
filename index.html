What we do

One service, done properly.

Pigeon Engineering installs smart control systems into existing residential garage door setups. You keep your door and, in most cases, your opener. You gain control from anywhere.

Smartphone Control

Open, close and check the status of your garage door from your phone, whether you are in the driveway or on holiday.

Smart Home Integration

Add your garage door to compatible ecosystems such as Apple Home and Google Home, alongside your lights and locks.

Professional Installation

Controller, sensors and wiring fitted with proper care for the layout and condition of your existing garage.

Clean, Seamless Finish

Considered cable routing and sensor placement, so the result looks intentional rather than added on.

Installation craft

The part you will see every day is the part we sweat over.

Every garage is different. Before anything is fixed in place, we plan where each component sits and how each cable runs, so the finished job preserves the look of your garage.

  1. Controller placementMounted where it has a good signal and is easy to reach for service, without being in the way.
  2. Routed, not dangledCables follow edges, rails and corners, secured and trimmed to length.
  3. Sensor positioningAligned and fixed for reliable readings, kept low-profile and tidy.
  4. Works with your motorConnected to your existing opener, with no change to how it operates day to day.
